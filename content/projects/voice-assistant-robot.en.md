Developed an AI robot that can naturally converse with users by combining natural language processing and speech recognition technologies.

<!--more-->

## Project Overview
- **Duration**: January 2022 - May 2022
- **Individual Project**: Graduation thesis project
- **Advisor**: Prof. Kim○○

## Tech Stack
- **Language**: Python
- **AI/ML**: PyTorch, Transformers, Whisper
- **Speech**: Google Speech-to-Text, gTTS
- **Robot**: ROS Melodic, TurtleBot3

## Core Features
1. **Speech Recognition System**
   - Utilized OpenAI Whisper model
   - Real-time speech-to-text conversion
   - Noise filtering and preprocessing

2. **Natural Language Understanding**
   - BERT-based intent classification
   - Named Entity Recognition (NER)
   - Conversation context management

3. **Conversation Generation**
   - GPT-2 based response generation
   - Emotion analysis and tone adjustment
   - Multi-turn conversation support

4. **Robot Behavior Control**
   - Movement according to voice commands
   - Gesture and LED expressions
   - Emotional state visualization

## Key Achievements
- Achieved 92% speech recognition accuracy
- Natural conversation satisfaction score of 4.2/5.0
- Won Excellence Award at graduation exhibition
