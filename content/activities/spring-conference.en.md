## Research Topic
**"Indoor Environment Robot Navigation System through Multi-Sensor Fusion"**

### Research Background
- Limitations of single sensor-based navigation
- Complexity of indoor environments and dynamic obstacle issues
- Need for fusion techniques that complement sensor advantages and disadvantages

### Proposed Method
1. **Sensor Configuration**
   - LiDAR: Accurate distance measurement
   - RGB-D camera: Visual information and depth
   - IMU: Inertial information correction

2. **Fusion Algorithm**
   - Extended Kalman Filter-based sensor fusion
   - Weight-based reliability evaluation
   - Real-time correction and updates

## Experimental Results
### Performance Comparison
- **LiDAR alone**: Position accuracy ±15cm
- **Camera alone**: Position accuracy ±25cm
- **Proposed method**: Position accuracy ±8cm

### Environmental Adaptability
- 30% improvement in robustness to lighting changes
- 25% improvement in dynamic obstacle recognition rate
- Overall navigation success rate of 92%
